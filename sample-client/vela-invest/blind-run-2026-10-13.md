## What this account needs before anything else (ranked by what is actually broken)

1. **Pull the two guaranteed-return headlines (SK_APP_acq_giveaway_2512 and PL_APP_acq_giveaway_2601) for compliance review.** These are live restricted claims in a regulated vertical, duplicated across markets, with EUR 180/day behind them. This is the highest legal and policy exposure. Per guardrail 12 the response is a proposal to pause the assets or campaign for the owner, not a delete. Each market needs its own independent compliance review, and PL needs a check on whether the giveaway mechanic is legal there.
2. **Fix the conversion setup.** The account optimises on App Install (counted "Every"). The value event, First Deposit, is Secondary in CZ/SK and absent in PL. The owner must confirm the intended value event, and the primary/secondary status then has to be corrected and read back. Until then every bid decision is based on the wrong signal.
3. **Close the PL attribution gap (61 vs 4) and add the missing PL First Deposit action.** The PL numbers cannot be trusted, and PL got the biggest recent spend increase. Check that the attribution link is shared into the PL sub-account, and run the same cross-check for CZ and SK.
4. **Retroactively document the 2026-01-14 PL budget increase.** It needs an approver, a success measure and a rollback trigger. Going forward, no budget change without a ledger row.
5. **Attach shared negatives to every campaign, starting with CZ_SEARCH_acq_generic_2409 (EUR 80/day).** Verify what "Vela - Master Negatives" contains. Review its search terms report.
6. **Review the old 2024-09 Search campaign's copy and positioning.** It has not been touched since creation.
7. **Rename the Polish-language asset headline to English.** Lowest severity, but a clear convention breach.
8. **Supply missing inputs to finish the audit:** goals.md (value event, target cost per activation, budgets), ad copy for the CZ campaigns, search terms export, CZ/SK attribution cross-checks, and conversion category and attribution model.
