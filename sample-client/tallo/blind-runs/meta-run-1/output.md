### Compliance detail

- **PL promo ads, both ad sets (`PL_acq_promo_2509_android` and `_ios`, EUR 145/day together), ad `PL_promo_v2`.** The copy says "Gwarantowany zysk 8% rocznie" (guaranteed 8 % return a year) for an ETF investment product. A guaranteed return on ETF portfolios is a restricted, almost certainly false financial claim (guardrail 4); `client-facts.csv` offers nothing that supports it, and the other markets' copy says value can fall. The same ad also runs a prize draw (iPhone 17, registration until 31.10) for a financial product. This is a mechanic that needs its own check under Polish lottery and consumer law and was not verified for PL (guardrail 10). The two issues are in the same ad, which is live now. Confidence: high on the claim being non-compliant, medium on the lottery rule (a lawyer needs to say). This is the most urgent finding. For the owner and compliance: a pause with a ledger row (pause, never delete; a pause is a proposal, guardrails 12 and 13).
- **Risk warning missing.** `CZ_winter_cozy_v1`, `SK_hook1_v1` and `SK_hook2_v1` have no "value may fall" line; `CZ_core_routine_v3`, `SK_hook3_v1` and `PL_dowod_spoleczny_v1` have one. Whether a risk line is mandatory in the ad text itself under the licence's marketing rules needs a compliance call (medium confidence); it is inconsistent either way.
- **Unsubstantiated claim:** "Ponad 100 000 osób inwestuje z Tallo" (`PL_dowod_spoleczny_v1`). No user number is in `client-facts.csv` (blocked); needs a source before it keeps running.
- **Licence vs advertiser:** the licence is held by Tallo s.r.o. and passported to PL, but PL ads run from Tallo Polska sp. z o.o. under its own portfolio and the Tallo s.r.o. card. Whether the Polish entity may advertise under the passport is a legal question for the owner (blocked).
- Per-market independent check (guardrail 9): the CZ and PL ads share the same structure ("risk line"), but PL promo is clearly not the product of a market-specific review.

1. **Stop the PL guaranteed-return and iPhone-draw ads from running unreviewed.** `PL_promo_v2` in both PL promo ad sets: needs the owner's decision (pause via an approved ledger row) and a compliance/legal check of the claim and the draw. EUR 145/day is spent on it now.

4. **Fix measurement before judging performance:** one install source (SDK or MMP, not both), and map `first_deposit` to a standard event. Question for the owner, with volume: First Deposit is 63 events a week across the whole account (about 3 % of installs), so say whether the account should keep optimizing on installs, and what the plan is, before any ad set can learn on it.
5. **Make changes go through the ledger.** Backfill rows for the +150 % PL budget jump, the 6 SK ad sets and the new CZ winter ad set, with an approver; Marek is an advertiser and the ledger only shows one change with an approver on record. Decide the review of the 2026-09-18 CZ budget row from real First Deposit cost.

7. **CZ winter ad set:** frequency 5.6 and 1-day click attribution, the only one that differs; align attribution before comparing, and check overlap with CZ core.
