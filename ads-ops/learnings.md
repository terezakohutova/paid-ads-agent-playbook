Status: `new` / `check` (verify in your own account first) / `decide` (contradicts a standing rule, costs money or adds a tool: the owner decides) / `test` (with budget, success number, rollback trigger) / `adopted <date>` / `rejected <date> why`.
Confidence: high = official docs or several independent sources; medium = one credible practitioner/vendor; low = one anecdote.
